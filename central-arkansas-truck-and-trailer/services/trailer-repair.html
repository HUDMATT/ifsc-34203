How Often Should Trailer Brakes Be Inspected?

Trailer brakes should be checked regularly, especially if the trailer is often loaded or used for long-distance trips. Wear varies depending on factors such as load weight, terrain, and driving habits. Regular inspections help prevent uneven braking, overheating, and longer stopping distances.

What Are Common Signs A Trailer Needs Repair?

Warning signs include uneven tire wear, pulling during braking, flickering or non-working lights, and unusual noises from suspension parts. You might also notice increased sway or less stability while driving. Catching these issues early helps prevent bigger damage and more downtime.

Can Preventive Maintenance Reduce Trailer Downtime?

Yes, preventive maintenance is crucial for trailer reliability. Regular inspections enable worn parts to be fixed or replaced before they break down on the road. This method helps extend component lifespan, enhances safety, and reduces unexpected service stops.
