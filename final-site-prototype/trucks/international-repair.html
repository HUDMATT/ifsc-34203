What Is The Diamond Logic Electrical System In International Trucks?

The Diamond Logic Electrical System controls lighting, safety interlocks, and body functions through programmable logic. When faults occur, multiple symptoms might appear simultaneously. Accurate diagnostics are crucial for identifying the root cause and restoring system communication.

How Does Predictive Cruising Work In International Trucks?

Predictive Cruising uses GPS data and vehicle inputs to anticipate terrain changes. It adjusts throttle and shifting before hills to improve efficiency. When sensors or software lose calibration, performance and fuel economy can decrease.
